(a letter to you).

Our last call shattered me. It made me realize that I have not moved on, I just covered my heart in protections. But underneath, I am still heartbroken, I am still grieving you like it was yesterday. Of course I need more time. Of course I need more slow, quiet time to find myself. I was always a little slower than you, I think, underneath.

I had all these layers that convinced me we don't make sense, and it was all so I didn't have to feel _this_. Because what am I meant to do if I still love you?

I have put myself in a real pickle, you know. I did my best. But somewhere in me, I think, couldn't fathom the thought of truly, really, being on my own. Even now when I touch it, it's like dying all over again. I know you don't think things are about death, but they are, for me. Little deaths, over and over, stepping heart first into the complete unknown. Like with leaving you, one tiny step at a time. One moment at a time. My heart is still learning to trust that the world is good.

I am learning, with horror, what it means to love with my heart wide open. My god. It is indescribably vulnerable, like a leaf grazed by every fingerprint, and yet also unimaginably strong, brave. Is it possible to open to being hurt, this much, and not die?

What if I want you, and I want you, and I want you, and I want you, and I cannot have you?
