I want to write what is true.

I went on Substack last week, for the first time in a while. I felt physically sick scrolling through the notes.

It's like, there is a level of posturing that is happening that is kind of unbelievable. One part of life is presented and the others aren't. And when I step into the energetic room of Substack, I can feel my system configuring into the Substack shape. Some parts of me come forward more, and others step back. I literally contort until I am the right shape to pass through.

I think it is because I don’t want to be vulnerable. Like, truly vulnerable. Like, I scrolled Instagram until 1am last night, and I’m scared that my coaching sessions with people might hurt them somehow, and I feel like a 5 year old in grown up boots.

I don’t want anyone to know that I feel like a 5 year old in grown up boots. But I have a feeling that until I can write from that place, my writing won’t feel fully authentic. It feels like … trying to throw a veil over everyone, saying something like: look, I have it all figured out! Isn’t that amazing. Look at the things I have figured out. But it’s not *real*. And I need to be real.

Like the only way to be in true connection is to out yourself constantly. Then it doesn’t need to be a role.

There’s a frame on Substack which is like, I am writing something for others – that others will read. Then there is, I am writing something for me, to share. Like, here world, I am offering you a window into myself. And there is a confusion about which one is happening, which one I should be doing. Is it okay to write a Substack that’s just saying: Here world, here I am. Or is there some way I have to kind of orient towards … here is something for you. A gift, that I’ve packaged, for you.

In basically no other circumstance am I doing this packaging thing. Every interaction I have with the people I love is just me being like ‘here I am’, and they love it. The messages I share with them are me being like, look, it’s me, expressing. And then somehow, there’s this field around Substack which is like … don’t just express. Express with a purpose, with a point, with some kind of moral or concept or thing that people want to hear.

Like, please only take up people’s time with your expression if it has a point, some deep insightful purpose.

So this is me saying: I am here to just express. You are so welcome to unfollow me, if you don’t want to receive my expression. I think sometimes my expression will be interesting, but it’s not here because it’s interesting, it’s here because it feels delightful to express and touch people with it.

It is so, so, so tempting when doing coaching / healing work to pull on the veneer of the role. Like truly, so tempting. Because it’s so fucking vulnerable to let the role fall away and just be a human. Like, here I am, a human, with all my messy stuff, and here you are, and you are giving me money to be somewhat emotionally healed?

But like, wholeness includes the messy. 

Feels like I’m just going to have to learn how to do this messily, and publicly.
